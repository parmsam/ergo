# Workstation setup

**Used in ergo:** the workstation checklist in the Guide, which also counts for 20% of the daily ergo score.
**Strength:** Expert guidance (`expert`), from agency and professional-society recommendations.

## What the guidance says

**Screen (OSHA [1], AAO [2])**
- Place the monitor directly in front of you, at least 20 in (50 cm) away. OSHA's preferred range is **20–40 in (50–100 cm)** [1]. The AAO suggests about **25 in**, roughly an arm's length [2].
- Put the top line of the screen at or slightly below eye level, so your gaze falls slightly downward [1, 2].
- Too far away and you lean in; too close and your eyes work harder [1].
- Place the monitor perpendicular to windows to reduce glare [1]; a matte screen filter can help [2].
- Match screen brightness to the room and increase contrast [2].

**Chair and desk (OSHA [1])**
- Lower back supported; feet flat on the floor or on a footrest.
- Keyboard and mouse close and at about elbow height, so shoulders stay relaxed and wrists stay straight.

## How ergo applies it

Each checklist item links here. Checking items off raises your setup percentage, which feeds into the ergo score. The score rewards fixing your setup once and then keeping up the breaks.

## Caveats

These are general guidelines. Bodies and tasks differ; adjust until it's comfortable, and change position often (see [posture-variety.md](posture-variety.md)).
