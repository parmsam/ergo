# Daily sitting budget

**Used in ergo:** the "Sitting (est.)" tile on Today, the sitting chart with a budget line on Progress, and a coach note when you approach the budget. The default budget is 8 h (configurable; 0 hides it).
**Strength:** Expert guidance (`expert`). A national guideline. Its developers rated it a strong recommendation, but based on very low-quality evidence.

## What the guidance says

- **Canadian 24-Hour Movement Guidelines for Adults (2020)** [1]:
  - Limit sedentary time to **8 hours or less** per day.
  - Within that, keep recreational screen time to **3 hours or less**.
  - Break up long periods of sitting **as often as possible**.
  - These are the first adult guidelines to set a number for sedentary time. The recommendation was graded *strong, very low-quality evidence* [2].
- **WHO 2020 guidelines** [3] recommend that adults limit sedentary time and replace it with activity of any intensity. They found the evidence insufficient to set a specific threshold.

## How ergo applies it

ergo can't see all of your sitting, so the number is an **estimate**:

> time ergo was running − time in body breaks − standing time you confirmed

Standing time counts only when you tap "I'm standing"; ergo then assumes you stood for the whole standing phase. Sitting away from the computer (meals, commuting, TV) isn't counted, so your real total is higher. The budget line is a reference, not a target to fill.

## Caveats

- The 8-hour figure is a guideline threshold, not a cliff: less sitting is better and any reduction helps.
- The estimate overstates sitting if you stand without confirming it, and understates it if you're seated while ergo is paused.
